# RAN Air Wing style guide

Derived from the sim's own interface (`index.html`). There was no written guide, so this one records what the sim already does. Blog, social images and any future pages follow it.

## Visual

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | `#0b1622` |
| Panel fill | `rgba(8,18,28,.62)` with 1px border `rgba(200,220,235,.18)`, radius 3px |
| Text | `#e8eef2` |
| Muted text | `#9fb4c2` (secondary), `#8fa6b5` (labels) |
| Accent (links, active, calls to action) | `#e8c547`; hover `#f3d86b`; text on accent `#10202c` |
| Success | `#5dff8f` |
| Warning / note | `#ff9a8c` |
| Type | `ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace`; body 15px / 1.7 on the blog, 12px in the sim HUD |
| Tracking | `.04em` body, `.12em`-`.16em` uppercase for headings and labels |
| Insignia | Australian White Ensign and the motto, see Insignia below. |
| Radius | 2-3px. No pills, no gradients, no drop shadows beyond a 1px text shadow over imagery |

Rules: dark background only; one accent colour; uppercase tracked labels for categories and metadata; key presses are shown as `<kbd>` in the accent colour; images are sim screenshots with a one-line caption stating camera, time and sea state.

## Insignia

The sim is set in 1977-1982, so the RAN's own ensign applies. The Australian White Ensign replaced the British White Ensign in RAN service on 1 March 1967.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Flag | Australian White Ensign, `assets/ensign.svg` (generated by `tools/brand/make_ensign.py`). Ratio 1:2; white field, Union Flag canton, blue Commonwealth Star in the lower hoist (outer radius 3/20 of the hoist, seven points), blue Southern Cross in the fly (four seven-pointed stars at 1/14 of the hoist, one five-pointed at 1/24). Star positions follow the national flag schedule. |
| Flag colours | Blue `#012169` (`--ensign-blue`), red `#e4002b` (`--ensign-red`), white `#ffffff`. These are the only places the guide uses red or white fills: they belong to the flag and are never used as UI colours. |
| Use | Always the whole flag, uncropped, unrotated and never recoloured. On the dark background keep the 1px border `rgba(200,220,235,.35)`. Minimum width 40px. Clear space of half the hoist. |
| Placement | Top left of an identity block: homepage title panel (48x24px, left of the ship name), the Open Graph image (150x75px, top left, labelled "Royal Australian Navy"), the About page when it is next edited. Not used as decoration or a background. |
| Motto | VIRES ACQUIRIT EUNDO ("She gathers strength as she goes"), set in uppercase tracked label type, muted `#8fa6b5` on the page and accent `#e8c547` on the Open Graph image. Always paired with the ship name, never on its own. |
| Honours | HMAS Melbourne (II) carries the battle honour Malaysia 1964-66 (the RAN's page gives these dates; some secondary sources say 1965-66) plus honours inherited from the earlier cruiser, Rabaul 1914 and North Sea 1916-18. Honours are set as plain label text, never as a graphic. |
| Ship's badge | The badge (a naval crown above a scroll lettered MELBOURNE, with the motto scroll below) is RAN heraldic artwork and is not reproduced here; the sources consulted do not describe laurels. If the owner holds or licenses the official badge, add it as `assets/badge.svg`, sized like the flag, and update this table. |
| Not used | The British White Ensign, the Union Flag alone, or any invented crest or wreath. |

Open Graph image: rebuilt by `python3 tools/brand/make_og.py` from `tools/brand/og-base.png` (the original artwork, unmodified), so the flag and text stay reproducible.

Sources: Royal Australian Navy Sea Power Centre, "HMAS Melbourne (II)" (seapower.navy.gov.au/node/5255); Australian National Maritime Museum blog, "The Australian White Ensign and its connection with HMAS Vampire" (via camd.org.au/?p=7268); Flags of the World, "Construction Details of the Australian Flag" (crwflags.com); Wikipedia, "Australian white ensign" and "HMAS Melbourne (R21)".

## Voice

- Plain, factual, Australian English (colour, metre, programme, defence).
- Written by a builder, not a brand: "I", "we" sparingly, never hype ("amazing", "epic").
- Say what is known, what is approximated, and what is not yet built. Mark approximations the way the sim notes do.
- Dates, names, figures and units are given exactly or not at all. Metric first with knots for speed; feet for altitude as the aircraft's instruments read.
- History claims need a source in the post's **Sources** list. Unsourced claims are cut.
- Respect the people. HMAS Melbourne's crews and the 1964 and 1969 collisions are told with care, not as spectacle.
- Short paragraphs. One idea each. Headings only when a post is longer than about 400 words.

## Imagery and rights

- Only screenshots from the sim, and original diagrams. Photographs in the reference folder belong to their owners (Australian War Memorial and others) and are not republished.
- Every post has a hero screenshot. Alt text describes what is visible, not the keyword.

## Post template

Title (sentence case, under 70 characters) / one-line summary (under 160) / hero screenshot + caption / body / "Fly it" call to action when relevant / Sources (history posts) / share buttons / category tags.
