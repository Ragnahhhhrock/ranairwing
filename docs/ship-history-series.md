# HMAS Melbourne ship-history series: strategy and 30-day calendar

Series run: Mon 16 Nov to Tue 15 Dec 2026, one post a day, Perth time. Prepared 15 Nov 2026.

## Strategy

**Goal.** Tell the life of HMAS Melbourne in order, from the 1942 design through construction, service and retirement, so a reader can follow the whole story and the sim's 1977-82 setting makes sense in context.

**Shape.** Five phases, 30 posts, strictly chronological. Each phase gets a recurring label so readers can browse it as a series.

| Phase | Days | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Inception and construction | 1-7 | 16-22 Nov |
| 2 Early service | 8-13 | 23-28 Nov |
| 3 Jet era | 14-19 | 29 Nov-4 Dec |
| 4 Late service | 20-25 | 5-10 Dec |
| 5 Retirement and afterlife | 26-30 | 11-15 Dec |

**Rules (carried over from the existing plan).**
- No history claim without a source. Every post lists its sources; the `Verify` column marks claims that rest on one site, or where sources disagree.
- Quote at most one short phrase per source. Paraphrase everything else.
- Reference photographs are never republished. Illustrate with sim screenshots only.
- Plain, factual Australian English. No hype.
- Fixed template: hook line, 150-250 words, a short 'Key facts' list, one 'In the sim' line only where the sim really has the thing, then sources.

**Sensitive posts (days 12, 16, 20).** The Voyager collision, the USS Frank E. Evans collision and the Albatross fire involve deaths or a crime. Use RAN or official wording, no sim tie-in, no promotion or calls to action, no emoji, moderate comments, and name no individuals. Do not schedule a light post's share push straight after these on social.

**Cadence.** One post a day. Times are randomised between 06:10 and 20:50 AWST, never repeated to the minute and at least an hour from the previous day's time, matching the existing rule. Anniversary hooks outside the daily post: reshare 6 Dec (first landing 1955), 24-26 Dec (Cyclone Tracy), 10 Feb 2027 (Voyager, respectful note only).

**Measures.** Per post: views, time on page, share clicks. Per phase: which posts send readers into the sim. Review after day 15 and day 30.

**Relationship to the 90-day plan.** These 30 posts are published as a second daily post on top of the existing one-a-day plan, with explicit `date:` front matter. Each time is at least an hour from the existing posts on the same, previous and next day, and none is on a 5-minute mark. The original schedule in `content/schedule.json` is unchanged.

## Post briefs

### Day 1: A carrier built to be thrown away
Mon 2026-11-16, 11:18 AWST. 1 Inception and construction. Category: Ship history.

**Hook.** Australia's last conventional carrier began life as a wartime design that was never meant to last.

**Key facts**
- Majestic class: one of six modified Colossus-class light fleet carriers ordered for the Royal Navy, with a stronger flight deck and lifts and a better catapult.
- The wartime '1942 design' programme treated these ships as disposable (scrapped after the war or within about three years of service).
- Melbourne began as HMS Majestic, lead ship of the class.

**Sources**
- https://seapower.navy.gov.au/node/5255
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMAS_Melbourne_(R21)
- https://naval-encyclopedia.com/cold-war/australia/hmas-melbourne.php

### Day 4: June 1947: Australia buys an unfinished carrier
Thu 2026-11-19, 19:14 AWST. 1 Inception and construction. Category: Ship history.

**Hook.** How the RAN ended up with a half-built ship, a sister ship and a loan carrier.

**Key facts**
- 3 June 1947: the Commonwealth approved acquiring two Majestic-class carriers; Melbourne (II) was one. Combined price for Majestic and Terrible was AU£2.75 million (funding cuts limited the RAN to two).
- 15 August 1947: government approved creating the Fleet Air Arm.
- Terrible commissioned as HMAS Sydney on 16 December 1948.
- HMS Vengeance was loaned to the RAN from 13 November 1952 to 12 August 1955 to cover the wait.

**Sources**
- https://seapower.navy.gov.au/node/5255
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMAS_Melbourne_(R21)

### Day 5: Three inventions bolted onto a wartime hull
Fri 2026-11-20, 17:26 AWST. 1 Inception and construction. Category: Carrier operations.

**Hook.** The angled deck, steam catapult and mirror landing sight changed carrier flying, and Melbourne got all three before she was finished.

**Key facts**
