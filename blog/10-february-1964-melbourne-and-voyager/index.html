10 February 1964: Melbourne and Voyager

27 November 2026 · 1 min read

Remembering HMAS Voyager and the 82 who died.

Remembering HMAS Voyager and the 82 who died.

  • About 8.56 pm, roughly 20 nautical miles south-east of Jervis Bay, during night flying exercises. Voyager was the plane guard.
  • Voyager broke in two; 82 died including all senior officers on her bridge; 232 survived. Melbourne picked up most survivors; 34 were rescued by Air Nymph and Air Sprite from HMAS Creswell.
  • Two royal commissions followed. The first faulted Melbourne's officers; the second (1967) cleared them.
