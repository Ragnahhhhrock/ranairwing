28 October 1955: two names in two days

21 November 2026 · 1 min read

She was commissioned as HMAS Majestic and renamed Melbourne almost immediately.

She was commissioned as HMAS Majestic and renamed Melbourne almost immediately.

RAN gives commissioning as 28 October 1955 under Captain Galfrey Gatacre; Wikipedia has her commissioned as Majestic on 26 October and renamed by Lady White two days later.

Namesake: the City of Melbourne. Motto: Vires acquirit eundo ('She gathers strength as she goes').
