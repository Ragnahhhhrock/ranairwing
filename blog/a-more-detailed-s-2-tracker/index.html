A more detailed S-2 Tracker

15 November 2026 · 2 min read

The Tracker model has been rebuilt from the reference photographs: a lofted fuselage, glazed cockpit, tapered wings, round engine nacelles and RAN markings.

The first Tracker was a tube with two wings and two propellers. I have rebuilt it from the reference photographs: the deck shots of the Trackers on Melbourne, the catapult launch, the landing approach and a close-up of an aircraft parked on the deck. The same model is used for the one you fly and the folded one that is towed around the deck.

What changed

  • Fuselage. A lofted, slightly squared oval section, grey over white, with a deeper belly. The cockpit is no longer a separate bubble: the roof curves into the top and sides of the fuselage, with a large side window each side, a windscreen and two small overhead windows set into the skin, each with a black frame, and a black anti-glare panel on the nose. A pilot and co-pilot now sit side by side in plain seats, visible through the glass. The Tracker was not fitted with ejection seats, so X does nothing in it except say so, and the on-screen key hints leave eject out. I have not added a bail-out in its place.
  • Wings and tail. Tapered wings with an airfoil section, a thin red line along the upper surface, flaps on the inboard trailing edge and navigation lights at the tips. The tailplane has an airfoil section too. The fin has a dorsal fillet. The outer panels still fold on the deck model.
  • Engines. Round nacelles with a cowl flap ring, a dark engine face, exhaust stacks, an oil cooler scoop and a spinner. The three-blade propellers now have tapered blades with white and red tips instead of yellow.
  • Markings. Kangaroo roundels on the fuselage and both upper wings, NAVY on the rear fuselage, a larger side number on the nose, a red band behind the cockpit, a small tail number and a gold badge on the fin.
  • Smaller parts. Underwing pylons, aerials, a pitot probe, a retracted search radome under the belly, a MAD stinger on the tail, a braced main gear with hubs and a twin nose wheel with a drag link.

Try it

Press N to switch to the Tracker, then C for the chase view and drag to orbit around it. G, F and H work the gear, flaps and hook as before.

Hold on: what is approximate

  • Proportions were judged by eye from photographs. I have not measured the model against a general arrangement drawing of the aircraft.
  • The fin badge is drawn from a photograph at low resolution. I have not confirmed which unit it belongs to, and the cat's head is a simplified version.
  • The grey is darker than the Skyhawk's on purpose, to read as grey in the sim's bright light. The exact paint shade is a guess.
  • The side number is still a placeholder.
  • The cockpit window edges are stair-stepped at close range, and the window shapes and positions are fitted by eye.
  • No weapons or stores are fitted, and the wheel bays and doors are not modelled.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
