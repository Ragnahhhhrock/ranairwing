Name tags over the ships

25 November 2026 · 1 min read

HMAS Melbourne, Perth, Derwent and Supply now carry a name tag overhead, like Pedro the plane guard helicopter, so you can tell which ship is which from a distance.

Pedro the plane guard helicopter has always had a callsign floating over it. The ships now get the same treatment: HMAS Melbourne, HMAS Perth, HMAS Derwent and HMAS Supply each carry a yellow name tag above the masts.

The tags fade in a short way from the ship and fade out again when she is a few kilometres off, so they do not clutter the deck camera or the horizon. Press O to cycle the orbit camera between the ships and you will see each one named.

Only the task group is tagged. Mission targets such as merchant ships and enemy contacts are left unlabelled, since finding them is part of those missions.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
