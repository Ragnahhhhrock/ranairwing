Confrontation, Vietnam and what Melbourne did not do

28 November 2026 · 1 min read

Melbourne spent the mid-1960s in South East Asia but never fought.

Melbourne spent the mid-1960s in South East Asia but never fought.

  • From 1965 to mid-1967: regular South East Asian deployments, including show-of-force exercises off Malaysia during the Indonesia-Malaysia Confrontation.
  • 1965-66: she escorted HMAS Sydney on short stretches of the troop voyages to Vietnam, stayed out of the Market Time area and took no part in combat.
  • She holds the battle honour Malaysia 1964-66 (the RAN's dates; some secondary sources say 1965-66).
