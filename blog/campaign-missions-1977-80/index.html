Campaign missions: two Tracker sorties from the 1977 and 1980 cruises

15 November 2026 · 1 min read

The mission list gets a dated Campaign group: a search for a disabled merchant ship (1977) and a shadowing sortie (1980), both flown in the Tracker.

The mission list now has a Campaign 1977-80 group. Each mission is named for a real event from Melbourne's cruises, and its card says what is documented and what the sim has invented.

C1, 25 May 1977: Search. On the voyage to the Silver Jubilee review, a Tracker from Melbourne found the disabled Dutch ship Impala Princess in the Gulf of Aden. In the sim you take off in the Tracker, fly about 24 km to a merchant hull, close to within 2 km below 1,500 m to confirm her, then land.

C2, October 1980: Shadowing the shadowers. During the Indian Ocean deployment, Soviet warships reportedly trailed the task group and a Tracker watched them. In the sim two contacts steam with the fleet. Stay within 4 km of one, below 1,500 m, for 60 seconds in total, then land.

How to try it. Open Missions (M). C1 unlocks after the T10 torpedo drop, and C2 after C1. The "unlock all missions" box skips that. Both use the Tracker, so the loadout is fixed and clean.

Still approximate - Both events rest on Wikipedia and the Naval Historical Society page, and the 1980 shadowing date is Wikipedia-only. Treat the history lines as reported, not confirmed. - The sim has no Gulf of Aden, no Dutch ship and no Soviet ships. The vessel and contacts are generic hulls placed 24-26 km from the carrier, and their courses are invented. - The contacts cannot be damaged and nothing is armed. Mission fuel is not simulated for the Tracker. - Not built yet: a Skyhawk campaign mission (the 1980 losses have no sourced details), and the Nowra shore start.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
