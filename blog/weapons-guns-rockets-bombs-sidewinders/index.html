Firing the guns, rockets, bombs and Sidewinders

15 November 2026 · 2 min read

Four weapons with different sights: a gun cross, a rocket and bomb pipper, and a Sidewinder seeker that only sees tailpipes.

Press I to cycle the weapon (GUN, AIM-9B, ROCKET, BOMB, depending on what you carry). The HUD shows the selected weapon and how many you have left.

Guns

Hold K. The gun cross marks where the two 20 mm cannon point. You have 200 rounds, and the shells drop under gravity, so aim a little high at range. Tracers show where they go.

Rockets

Hold K to ripple rockets from your pods. The pipper shows where a rocket fired now would land. 2.75 in pods hold 19 rockets and 5 in pods hold 4.

Bombs

Press K to release one bomb. The pipper shows where it would land if released now. Against a barge, a 500 lb bomb kills within about 25 m and a 250 lb bomb within about 18 m.

Sidewinders

The AIM-9B was an early missile, and the sim treats it that way. The seeker looks only in a 10 degree cone ahead, out to 4 km, and only sees a target pointing away from you. Get behind the drone, put it inside the circle, wait for the LOCK cue and tone, then press K. The missile flies up to 650 m/s, steers on the target, and gives up if the target leaves its field of view. A hit within about 15 m destroys the drone.

The HUD draws a bracket and range on every target, with an arrow at the screen edge when one is off to the side.

Try it

Missions T4 to T7 teach each weapon in turn.

Still approximate

  • Muzzle and rocket speeds, the rate of fire, and the lethal radii are game values.
  • Drones and barges are generic. Nothing models real damage, fuzes or the AIM-9B's real seeker limits.
  • Rockets and bombs fall on a flat sea with no wind. The pipper ignores your own manoeuvring.
  • Bombs and rockets cannot hit the ship, the escorts or the tanker.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
