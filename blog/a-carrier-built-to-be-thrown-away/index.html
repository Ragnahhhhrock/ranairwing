A carrier built to be thrown away

16 November 2026 · 1 min read

Australia's last conventional carrier began life as a wartime design that was never meant to last.

Australia's last conventional carrier began life as a wartime design that was never meant to last.

Majestic class: one of six modified Colossus-class light fleet carriers ordered for the Royal Navy, with a stronger flight deck and lifts and a better catapult.

Melbourne began as HMS Majestic, lead ship of the class, and was never commissioned into the Royal Navy.
