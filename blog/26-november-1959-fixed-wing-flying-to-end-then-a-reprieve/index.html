On this date in 1959 the Minister announced fixed-wing naval aviation would end in 1963.

  • Wessex helicopters were ordered in its place.
  • In mid-1963 the government let the Sea Venoms and Gannets stay until at least 1967.
  • Milestones in the same years: 10,000th catapult launch (late 1962) and 20,000th landing (17 March 1963).
