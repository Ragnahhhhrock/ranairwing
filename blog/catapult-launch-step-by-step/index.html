Catapult launch, step by step

25 November 2026 · 1 min read

Six steps from the catapult to level flight at 1,000 ft, with the reasons behind each.

Press R to start on the catapult, or U for the guided version. The aircraft begins in the launch configuration: gear down, flaps half, hook up.

1. Run the engine to 100%

Hold = until RPM reads 100%. If you press Z early, the sim tells you to run up to full power first. The launch is refused until the engine is above about 85%.

2. Launch

Press Z. The catapult stroke is over in under two seconds. Leave the stick alone while it runs.

3. Climb away

Hold ↓ for a second or two. The aircraft holds that climb when you let go.

4. Raise the gear

Press G once you are climbing and clear of the ship.

5. Retract the flaps

Press F until the HUD shows FLAP UP. From half, that is two presses: half, full, up.

6. Level off

Climb to 1,000 ft, tap ↑ to level, and hold - to bring power back to about 40% so you stop accelerating. Level flight means the climb figure under FEET reads near zero.

Why a catapult

A carrier's deck is too short for a jet to take off on its own power. The catapult gives it flying speed in a short run. Wind over deck adds to that speed, which is why the ship turns into the wind: see wind over deck.

If nothing happens

Check that RPM is above 85%. Below that the launch is refused.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
