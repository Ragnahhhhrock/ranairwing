Seven cameras and when to use each

23 November 2026 · 1 min read

Orbit, Deck, Island, Approach, Flyby, Cockpit and Chase: what each camera is for.

Keys 1 to 7 switch cameras. Each one is useful for something different.

Key Camera Use it for
1 Orbit Looking at the whole ship. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
2 Deck From the stern end of the flight deck, looking forward along it.
3 Island From the island, looking aft over the deck toward the wires.
4 Approach A camera that glides in along the glidepath to the angled deck, then starts over.
5 Flyby A fixed point out at sea that the ship steams past, zoomed in.
6 Cockpit The pilot's view with the HUD. Hardest, most rewarding.
7 Chase Behind the aircraft. Best for learning.

C swaps between cockpit and chase.

Which to fly in

Start in chase. You can see the aircraft's attitude and bank from behind, and the HUD stays on screen. Once landings are steady, move to the cockpit for the pilot's view.

When you restart with R or T, the sim moves you to chase unless you are already in a flying camera.

Camera hints

  • The orbit camera stays centred on the ship, not your aircraft. Switch to chase or cockpit when you launch.
  • The Approach camera is a good way to see what a correct glidepath looks like before you fly one.
  • In the Deck and Island views you can drag to look around.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
