Weapons now have sound: guns, rockets, bombs and torpedoes

16 November 2026 · 1 min read

Firing and releasing stores now makes noise, from the 20 mm cannon to a depth charge going off under the sea.

Until now most weapons were silent. Rockets and the Sidewinder shared one hiss, and bombs, torpedoes and depth charges made no sound when released. Each weapon now has its own sound, and so do the impacts.

What you hear

  • Cannon: a short crack with a low thump, repeating while you hold Space.
  • Rockets: an ignition crack, then the motor whoosh, on every rocket.
  • AIM-9B: a thump as it leaves the rail, then a longer motor roar.
  • Bombs, torpedoes and depth charges: a clunk as the store leaves the rack.
  • Jettison (J): a heavier clunk of the racks letting go.
  • Impacts: a splash when a torpedo, rocket or jettisoned store hits the sea, a bang for bombs and kills, and a muffled thump under the splash for a depth charge.

How to try it

Press I to choose a weapon and Space to fire or release. J jettisons everything. M turns sound off and on.

What is approximate

All of these are synthesised in the browser from noise and tones, the same way the deck sounds are. They are game sounds, not recordings of the real weapons. Sounds do not change with distance from the impact yet.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
