Tracker torpedoes, depth charges, practice bombs and visible rockets

15 November 2026 · 2 min read

The S-2 Tracker gets its own loadout, torpedo and depth charge release and a submarine to hit; the Skyhawk gets practice bomb racks and rockets you can see.

Press N to switch to the Tracker, then V for its loadout screen.

Tracker stores

The sources say the Tracker carried two homing torpedoes, two depth bombs or four depth charges in the bay, and rocket pods, torpedoes or 250 lb bombs on six wing points, plus sonobuoys. The sim follows that:

  • Eight stations: six wing points and two bay positions. Torpedoes go anywhere; depth bombs and depth charge pairs go in the bay; 250 lb bombs and Zuni pods go on the wing.
  • Presets: ASW patrol, ASW with four torpedoes, depth charges, strike.
  • I cycles TORPEDO, DEPTH CHG, BOMB and ROCKET; K releases; J jettisons.
  • Wing stores are drawn on the airframe. Bay stores are not drawn until released, because the bay doors are not modelled.

A submarine to hit

Mission T10 puts you in the Tracker over a snorkelling submarine (sail, periscope and wake). A torpedo kills it if it lands within about 150 m; a depth charge within about 20 to 25 m. The torpedo does not home or run: it is a lethal radius around where it splashes, so lead the target as you would with a bomb. Torpedoes do not damage surface targets.

Skyhawk additions

  • A practice bomb rack (six small blue practice bombs). A 1974 photo caption says 886 sat on the ramp with practice bombs and drop tanks. The rack type and the bomb size are not sourced. The Practice bombing preset carries four racks and a centreline tank.
  • Rockets in flight are now drawn, not just trails: a 5 in Zuni-sized rocket from the LAU-10 pods and a smaller one from the 2.75 in pods.

What is estimated

Every size and weight for the new stores is an estimate (torpedo, depth bomb, depth charge, practice bomb and rack, rockets). The sources name the weapon types, not their dimensions. The Sea King Mk 50 is not flyable and no source gives its rack positions, so it has no stores yet; the sources say it carried Mk 46 torpedoes and depth charges.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
