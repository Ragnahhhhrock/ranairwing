How to fly

Keyboard controls cheat sheet

20 November 2026 · 1 min read

Every key in the sim on one page: flight controls, cameras, and the commands for starting, stopping and restarting.

Flying

Key Action
↑ ↓ or W S Stick. Down pulls back and the nose rises.
← → or A D Roll
Q E Rudder
= / - Throttle up and down (also Page Up / Page Down and ] / [)
G Gear up and down
F Flaps: cycles half, full, up
H Arrestor hook up and down
B Speedbrakes
Z Launch from the catapult
Space Fire the selected weapon in the air, and wheel brakes on deck
X twice Eject. The first press arms it for about a second and a half.

Starting and stopping

Key Action
R Restart on the catapult
T Start an approach, 1.2 nm astern
U Start the guided tutorial
Enter Next lesson, when a lesson has finished
Esc Leave the tutorial
M Sound on and off
L Raise or lower the aft lift. Locked out while aircraft are airborne.

Cameras

Keys 1 to 7 select Orbit, Deck, Island, Approach, Flyby, Cockpit and Chase. C switches between cockpit and chase. In the orbit-type cameras, drag to look and scroll to zoom. More in seven cameras and when to use each.

Hints

  • G only moves the gear when you are airborne. It does nothing on the deck.
  • Z launches from the catapult once the engine is at full power; below that the sim reminds you to run up first. Space is the wheel brakes on deck and fires the selected weapon in the air.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
