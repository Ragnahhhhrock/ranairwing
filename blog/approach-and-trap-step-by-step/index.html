Approach and arrested landing, step by step

28 November 2026 · 1 min read

Eight steps from 3.5 nm astern at 220 kn to a wire on the angled deck.

Press U and then Enter after the launch lesson for the guided version, or follow this list. You begin 3.5 nm astern at 800 ft and 220 kn: far too fast to land, with the ship straight ahead.

1. Slow down

Hold - to idle and press B for speedbrakes. Hands off the stick: the aircraft holds level.

2. Gear down

Below 200 kn, press G. Watch KNOTS on the left of the HUD.

3. Flaps full

Press F twice.

4. Hook down

Press H. No hook, no trap.

5. Settle at 120 to 125 kn

At 140 kn press B to retract the speedbrakes, then hold = to about 35% power.

6. Hold speed and wait for the ball

Stay level at 120 to 125 kn. The ball starts below the datum and rises as you close on the ship. See the ball.

7. Ball centred: start down

Tap ↑ to start descending and ease the power to about 28%. Aim for 500 to 700 fpm down.

  • Ball high: tap ↑ or reduce power.
  • Ball low: tap ↓ or add power.
  • Use small taps of ← → for line-up. The wings level themselves.

8. Short final

Keep the ball centred and fly the aircraft onto the deck. Do not flare. A red ball means you are dangerously low: add power and pull up.

What happens next

If the hook catches a wire, the sim reports which one and the sink rate, and pulls the throttle to idle as the wire stops you. If it misses, you have bolted: see arrestor wires, bolters and the hook.

Launch from the catapult, fly the pattern and land on the angled deck, in your browser.
