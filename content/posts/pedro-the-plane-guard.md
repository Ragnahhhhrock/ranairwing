The Sea King that hovers astern of Melbourne during flying now has a name. A small yellow PEDRO tag floats above it.

Pedro is the nickname for the rescue helicopter on station while fixed-wing aircraft launch and recover. The sim already had the helicopter holding station off the port quarter, rotor turning and drifting slightly in the swell. This change adds the tag so you can pick it out and know what it is for.

## Try it

Orbit camera (<kbd>1</kbd>), then drag round to the port quarter. The tag is clear within a few hundred metres and fades out as you pull away, so it does not clutter the horizon.

## Still approximate

- Pedro is only a name tag and a hover. It does not respond to crashes or ejections, and you cannot call it up or fly it yet.
- Position, height and drift are game values, not taken from a procedure.
- The nickname comes from the owner; there is no source list for it in this post.
