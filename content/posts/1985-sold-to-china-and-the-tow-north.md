A tow that nearly didn't make it out of Queensland waters.

- February 1985: sold to China United Shipbuilding for A$1.4 million; weapons and electronics removed and rudders welded in place; catapult, arresting gear and landing system left aboard.
- 27 April 1985: left Sydney under tow by De Ping. 30 April: tow line parted, shelter in Moreton Bay. About 3 May: ran aground in Moreton Bay.
- 13 June 1985: arrived at Huangpu near Guangzhou.
