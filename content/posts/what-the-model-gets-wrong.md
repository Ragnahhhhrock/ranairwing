Every model simplifies. These are the places where this one is known to be approximate, so nobody has to find them the hard way.

## Ship

- **Flight deck outline and angled deck geometry.** The angled deck is drawn at 5.5 degrees. The plan drawings were not measured precisely, so the exact outline is an estimate.
- **Arrestor wires.** Five are modelled. The drawings were not clear enough to confirm the count or spacing.
- **Hull sides.** Galleries, boat bays and gun sponsons are simplified.
- **Marking colours and positions for 1980.** Based on photographs, which vary.
- **Deck crew jerseys.** The colours follow an approximate colour code and have not been checked against RAN practice of the period.
- **Sea King and S-2 Tracker models.** New, and checked only against the reference photographs.

## Aircraft

- **Flight model.** Game-grade. It is tuned to be flyable on a keyboard, not to match real handling.
- **Numbers and individual markings.** See the [livery post](/blog/a-4g-livery-and-markings/).

## Sea and weather

- **Wind direction.** Always along the ship's track.
- **Sea state.** Seven levels, with wave-driven pitch, roll and heave, but not a model of any specific sea.

## How to help

If you know the ship, a correction is worth more than a compliment. Email [contact@ranairwing.com](mailto:contact@ranairwing.com) or open an issue on the [project's GitHub page](https://github.com/Ragnahhhhrock/ranairwing/issues), and say what is wrong and, if you can, where to check. Where a correction can be verified, the model is changed and the fix is described in a post.
