You can fly a full launch and landing in a few minutes. The built-in tutorial does the teaching, so there is nothing to read first.

## Step 1: open the sim and press U

The tutorial panel appears at the top of the screen. It tells you which key to press and waits until the aircraft shows you have done it, then moves to the next step.

{{shot:s20-tutorial|Lesson 1, step 1: the tutorial panel names the key to press and waits for the aircraft to respond.}}

## Step 2: lesson 1, catapult launch

Six steps. You run the engine up to 100%, press <kbd>Space</kbd> to launch, climb away, raise the gear, retract the flaps and level off at 1,000 ft.

## Step 3: lesson 2, approach and arrested landing

Eight steps. You start 3.5 nm astern at 800 ft and 220 kn, slow down, configure the aircraft for landing, then fly the ball down to the deck. When the hook catches a wire, the tutorial ends.

## If you want to skip the tutorial

- <kbd>R</kbd> starts you on the catapult.
- <kbd>T</kbd> starts you on a ready-made approach, 1.2 nm astern with gear, flaps and hook already down.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves the tutorial and returns to free flight.

## Tips

- The keys are on screen in the sim. A full list is in the [controls cheat sheet](/blog/keyboard-controls/).
- A first landing often ends in a bolter or a crash. That is normal. See [ten mistakes new pilots make](/blog/ten-mistakes-new-pilots-make/).
- Use the chase camera (<kbd>7</kbd>) until you are comfortable, then try the cockpit (<kbd>6</kbd>).
