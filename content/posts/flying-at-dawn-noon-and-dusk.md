The Time slider at the bottom right runs from 05:30 to 18:30 in 15-minute steps. The default is 15:30.

## What it changes

- **Sun height.** Up to about 62 degrees at midday. Near the ends of the range the sun is on or below the horizon.
- **Sun direction.** It tracks across the ship's port side as the day goes on.
- **Sky and haze.** Blue at midday, with warm light and orange haze near the horizon.

## What to try

- **Midday.** Hard light, the clearest view of the deck markings. Best for learning.
- **Early morning and late afternoon.** Warm, low light. The best for screenshots.
- **The ends of the range.** Twilight. It is dim and the deck is hard to read.

## Is it night flying?

No. The slider stops at 05:30 and 18:30, and night operations are not modelled. Night operations were a distinct part of carrier flying, with their own lighting and procedures, and could be a later addition. See the [roadmap](/blog/roadmap-eras-and-aircraft/).
