The RAN's first jets with a real fighter-bomber punch arrive by carrier.

- The first A-4G flew on 19 July 1967.
- Melbourne sailed from Sydney on 20 September 1967; aircraft embarked at San Diego on 27 and 30 October.
- Skyhawks landed at Jervis Bay and Trackers at Sydney on 21 and 22 November 1967.
- The first order was eight single-seat A-4Gs and two TA-4G trainers. The two-seaters could not operate from Melbourne.
- The A-4G could not launch guided air-to-ground weapons.
