The sim's ship was built to a 1969 general arrangement drawing of HMAS Melbourne, plus photographs. These are the figures printed on that drawing.

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Length overall | 213.8 m |
| Length between perpendiculars | 194.0 m |
| Hull beam | 24.4 m |
| Flight deck width (maximum) | 38.4 m |
| Draught | 7.6 m |
| Standard displacement | 16,000 tons |
| Deep load displacement | 19,950 tons |
| Power | 40,000 shp |
| Speed | 24.5 kn |
| Guns listed | Twelve 40 mm |

Other publications may give slightly different numbers, because ships change during their service and sources measure differently. These are the figures the model was built from, and any history post that quotes a number will say where it comes from.

## What the numbers mean for the sim

- **Speed.** The ship-speed slider runs from 0 to 24 kn, just under the drawing's 24.5 kn. The default is 20 kn.
- **Beam against deck width.** The flight deck, at up to 38.4 m, is much wider than the 24.4 m hull. The deck overhangs the hull, most visibly at the bow and along the angled deck.

## What the sim does not show

The twelve 40 mm guns on the drawing are only hinted at in the model. Gun sponsons, galleries and boat bays along the hull sides are simplified. A later post lists what the model still gets wrong.
