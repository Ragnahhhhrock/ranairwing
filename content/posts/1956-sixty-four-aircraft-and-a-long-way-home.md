The maiden voyage from Scotland to Sydney with three squadrons embarked.

Left Glasgow 11 March 1956 via the Suez Canal carrying 64 aircraft of 808, 816 and 817 Squadrons.

Reached Fremantle on 23 April and Sydney on 9 May 1956 (RAN dates).
