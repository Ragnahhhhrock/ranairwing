Each of these ends a flight early. Each is fixable.

1. **Launching before full power.** The sim refuses and tells you to run up first. Hold <kbd>=</kbd> until RPM is 100%.
2. **Forgetting the hook.** Press <kbd>H</kbd> on approach. With the hook up you get "Hook up: touch and go" and a go-around.
3. **Flaring.** Do not pull the nose up just before touchdown. The deck is not a runway. Fly it onto the deck.
4. **Chasing the ball with big stick inputs.** Use small taps of <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> and wait to see the result.
5. **Ignoring line-up.** Your landing line is the angled deck, not the ship's centreline. Watch for LINED UP under the ball.
6. **Arriving banked.** More than about 21 degrees of bank at touchdown is a wing strike, and fatal.
7. **Wrong attitude at touchdown.** More than about 25 degrees nose-up is a tail strike. More than about 10 degrees nose-down is a nose-first touchdown.
8. **Too much sink.** A sink rate over about 1,570 fpm collapses the gear. Aim for 500 to 700 fpm on the glidepath.
9. **Wrong speed.** On approach, hold 120 to 125 kn, with the amber doughnut lit on the indexer. Red chevron means fast, green means slow.
10. **Speedbrakes left out.** The tutorial has you retract them at 140 kn. Check the BRK flag on the HUD.

## If you keep crashing

Run the [guided tutorial](/blog/the-guided-tutorial/). It sets up the approach, tells you each step, and restarts it if you bolt. After that, return to free flight.

Each landing is a sequence. Every one of these mistakes is a step done late, early or not at all.
