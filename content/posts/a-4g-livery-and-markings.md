The reference photographs show the RAN's A-4G in a consistent scheme. These are the features the sim aims for.

## What the photos show

- **Colours.** Light gull grey over white.
- **Rudder.** A red and white checkerboard.
- **Rear fuselage.** "NAVY" in capitals.
- **National marking.** The kangaroo roundel.
- **Side numbers.** In the 870s to 880s.

## What the sim has now

The model carries all five. The upper surfaces are light grey with a white underside, the rudder is a red and white checkerboard, "NAVY" is painted on the rear fuselage on both sides, and roundels appear on the wings and fuselage. The side number is 885.

Every Skyhawk in the sim wears 885 for now, including the three spares parked forward of the island, because they are copies of the same model. Giving each aircraft its own number is an obvious next step.

## What is still approximate

- **Exact colours and positions for 1980.** Photographs of the period differ in how faded the paint looks, and colour in old prints is unreliable.
- **Individual side numbers.** As above.
- **Weathering.** The aircraft are clean. Real carrier aircraft were not.
- **Small markings.** Stencilling, walkway lines, warning triangles and unit badges are not modelled.

## Other types in the references

The same folder shows the Sea King Mk 50 in grey with two-digit nose numbers, and the S-2 Tracker also embarked. Both are candidates for later phases. See [beyond the Skyhawk](/blog/beyond-the-skyhawk-the-air-wing/).
