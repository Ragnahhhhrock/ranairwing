The Sea state slider runs from 0 to 6. It changes three things at once.

## What changes

- **The waves.** Larger swell, and with it more pitch, roll and heave of the ship. The readouts at bottom left show the numbers.
- **The wind.** From 2 kn at sea state 0 to 32 kn at sea state 6. See [wind over deck](/blog/wind-over-deck/).
- **The glidepath.** The ball is measured against the ship's height, so it moves with the ship's heave.

## What to expect

At low sea states the ship is nearly still and the deck is a steady target. At higher ones the PITCH, ROLL and HEAVE readouts show how much the deck is moving, and you can see it in the camera views.

## Tips

- Use the orbit camera first to watch how the ship moves before you fly.
- Raise the ship speed to 24 kn. It increases the wind over deck, which helps launches.
- Keep the same discipline as in calm weather: stable speed, small corrections. In heavy seas small is even more important.

## Limits of the model

The deck's pitch and roll are shown but the ball is only corrected for heave. A real landing aid was stabilised against ship motion, and the sim does not model that in detail yet. The [approximations post](/blog/what-the-model-gets-wrong/) lists other simplifications.
