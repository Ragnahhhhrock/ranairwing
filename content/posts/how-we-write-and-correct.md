This blog tells the history of a real ship and the people who served in her. That brings a duty to get it right.

## History

- **Sources.** Every history post ends with a list of the sources it used. Official records and published histories come first.
- **Exact or not at all.** A date, name or figure is given exactly or not at all.
- **Uncertainty is said out loud.** If sources disagree, the post says so.
- **Respect.** Accidents and losses are told plainly and with care.
- **No long quotes.** Short quotations only, with a link to the source.

## Images

Every image is a screenshot from the sim. Photographs belong to their owners and are not republished. Each caption says the camera, the time and the sea state.

## The sim

Where the sim simplifies something, the post says so. The [approximations post](/blog/what-the-model-gets-wrong/) is kept up to date.

## Corrections

If you find an error, email [contact@ranairwing.com](mailto:contact@ranairwing.com) or open an issue on the [project's GitHub page](https://github.com/Ragnahhhhrock/ranairwing/issues). When a correction is confirmed:

1. The post is fixed.
2. The post carries a note saying what changed and when.
3. If the sim was wrong too, it is fixed and a dev diary post describes the change.

## Tone

Plain, factual, Australian English. No hype.
