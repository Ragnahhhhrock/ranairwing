Rough weather and some good-natured nicknames.

- March to July 1978: RIMPAC 78. She earned the nickname 'Little M' working with USS Enterprise.
- 3 March 1979: a boiler explosion caused minor damage during refit; refit ended 3 August 1979.
- Late 1979: heavy seas swept an LW-02 radar aerial and Skyhawk N13-154907 overboard.
