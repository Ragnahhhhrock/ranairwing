The sim has a historical setting: 1977 to 1982. The title panel says "c. 1980", the middle of that window.

## The end date

HMAS Melbourne was decommissioned in June 1982. A sim set after that would have no ship to fly from, so the setting ends there.

## One air wing, done properly

The ship's air wing was very different in earlier years: other aircraft, other squadrons, other routines. Each period would need its own aircraft models, markings and checking against references. Choosing the final years means the project starts with one air wing to get right, built around the A-4G Skyhawk, with the S-2 Tracker and Sea King as later candidates. Those three types are in the reference photographs.

## What the reference photos cover

The folder of reference images spans the ship's service from 1956 to 1980. That gives the model plenty to check against, though the markings and fit of the ship changed over those years, and the sim shows one version. The [approximations post](/blog/what-the-model-gets-wrong/) says which details are uncertain.

## Could there be other eras?

Perhaps. An option to play different periods, when the air wing was very different, is an idea on the list. It depends on having good enough references for each era. See the [roadmap](/blog/roadmap-eras-and-aircraft/).

## A note on dates

Where a post says something happened in a given year, it cites a source. The 1977 to 1982 window is a design choice for the sim, not a claim about when the ship was at her best.
