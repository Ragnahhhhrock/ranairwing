Press <kbd>U</kbd> and then <kbd>Enter</kbd> after the launch lesson for the guided version, or follow this list. You begin 3.5 nm astern at 800 ft and 220 kn: far too fast to land, with the ship straight ahead.

## 1. Slow down

Hold <kbd>-</kbd> to idle and press <kbd>B</kbd> for speedbrakes. Hands off the stick: the aircraft holds level.

## 2. Gear down

Below 200 kn, press <kbd>G</kbd>. Watch KNOTS on the left of the HUD.

## 3. Flaps full

Press <kbd>F</kbd> twice.

## 4. Hook down

Press <kbd>H</kbd>. No hook, no trap.

## 5. Settle at 120 to 125 kn

At 140 kn press <kbd>B</kbd> to retract the speedbrakes, then hold <kbd>=</kbd> to about 35% power.

## 6. Hold speed and wait for the ball

Stay level at 120 to 125 kn. The ball starts below the datum and rises as you close on the ship. See [the ball](/blog/the-ball/).

## 7. Ball centred: start down

Tap <kbd>↑</kbd> to start descending and ease the power to about 28%. Aim for 500 to 700 fpm down.

- Ball high: tap <kbd>↑</kbd> or reduce power.
- Ball low: tap <kbd>↓</kbd> or add power.
- Use small taps of <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> for line-up. The wings level themselves.

## 8. Short final

Keep the ball centred and fly the aircraft onto the deck. Do not flare. A red ball means you are dangerously low: add power and pull up.

## What happens next

If the hook catches a wire, the sim reports which one and the sink rate, and pulls the throttle to idle as the wire stops you. If it misses, you have bolted: see [arrestor wires, bolters and the hook](/blog/wires-bolters-and-the-hook/).
