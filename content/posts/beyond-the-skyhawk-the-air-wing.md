An aircraft carrier is more than its fighters. The air wing is a team of different aircraft with different jobs. The sim has two that you can fly and one more that you can see.

## Flyable today

**A-4G Skyhawk.** A catapult launch and an arrested landing. Three spares are parked on deck.

**S-2 Tracker.** The same launch and landing in a slower propeller aircraft. Press <kbd>N</kbd> to switch. See [Fly the S-2 Tracker](/blog/fly-the-s-2-tracker/).

## On deck, not flyable

- **S-2 Tracker (second one).** A Tracker is also towed to the forward lift and back with its outer wings folded up and back. That one is part of the deck scene; the one you fly is separate.
- **Sea King Mk 50.** One parked on helicopter spot 1 with its blades folded, and one hovering off the port quarter on plane guard. Grey, with a five-blade main rotor.

All three types appear in the reference photographs. The Tracker and Sea King models are new and have only been checked against those photographs.

## What a flyable helicopter would add

- **A helicopter.** A different way of flying altogether, with hover and the numbered deck spots.
The Tracker is already flyable. The helicopter is not planned for a particular date, and nothing is promised.

## What the blog will do

Each type gets its own aircraft profile with sourced history: when it served, how many were in service, what it did aboard the ship. Those posts are in the schedule and will be written once the sources are checked. Until then, nothing about them is claimed here beyond what the photographs show.
