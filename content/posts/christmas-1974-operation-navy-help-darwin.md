Cyclone Tracy flattened Darwin on 24-25 December 1974 and Melbourne was sent north.

- Sailed 26 December 1974; left Darwin 18 January 1975.
- Acted as operational headquarters and helicopter base.
- Her Wessex helicopters flew 2,493 sorties, carrying 7,824 passengers and 107 tons of cargo.
