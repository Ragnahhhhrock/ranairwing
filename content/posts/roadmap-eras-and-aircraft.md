Here is where the sim could go next. These are ideas, not commitments.

## Already done

- The carrier at sea, with sea states and a time-of-day slider.
- A flyable A-4G and a flyable S-2 Tracker, each with a catapult launch and an arrested landing.
- A guided tutorial with two lessons.
- Seven cameras, a HUD and sound.
- Deck life: crew in coloured jerseys that react to launches, working aircraft lifts, an S-2 Tracker being towed to the forward lift, and Sea Kings on deck and on plane guard.

## Ideas

**Aircraft**
- A flyable Sea King helicopter.
- Individual side numbers on each Skyhawk.

**Eras**
- An option to play different periods of the ship's life, when the air wing was very different. This needs good references for each period.

**Realism**
- Wind that does not always blow straight down the ship.
- Night operations.
- Better hull detail: galleries, boat bays and gun sponsons.

**Teaching**
- A lesson on go-arounds.
- A lesson on flying the full circuit.

## How things get chosen

Three things decide the order: how much it improves the main experience of launching and landing, how well it can be checked against references, and what players ask for. Corrections to existing details come before new features.

## How you find out

Every new feature gets a post on this blog when it ships. The [RSS feed](/blog/feed.xml) has them all.
