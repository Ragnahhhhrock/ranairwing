A$8.5 million and fourteen months in Garden Island dock.

- December 1967 to February 1969: refit at Garden Island to operate S-2 Trackers and A-4G Skyhawks.
- Wikipedia gives the cost as A$8.5 million, with Bofors guns removed, the Type 277Q radars replaced by an LW-02 air search radar and an SPN-35 landing aid radar, and TACAN and ECM pods fitted.
- The ship was rededicated on 14 February 1969, and made her 1,000th landing since the refit on 29 April 1969.
