A keel laid in wartime Barrow-in-Furness for a ship the Royal Navy would never commission.

- Laid down as HMS Majestic by Vickers-Armstrongs at Barrow-in-Furness on 15 April 1943.
- The sim's hull is built from a 1969 general arrangement drawing: 213.8 m overall, 24.4 m hull beam, 7.6 m draught, 40,000 shp and 24.5 kn.
- Displacement differs by source. The drawing says 16,000 tons standard and 19,950 deep load; the Sea Power Centre gives 15,740 tons standard and 20,000 tons full load.
