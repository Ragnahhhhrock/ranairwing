Melbourne takes over as RAN flagship and spends her first year working.

- Became flagship in mid-May 1956, replacing HMAS Sydney (RAN: 12 May).
- September to November 1956: first South East Asian deployment, including Exercise Albatross.
- Mid-November 1956: about 200 of her crew supported the Melbourne Olympics.
