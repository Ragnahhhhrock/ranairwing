After Phase 1, the model was checked against a folder of twenty reference files: two profile-and-plan drawings, ship photographs from 1956 to 1980, and pictures of the A-4G, S-2 Tracker and Sea King on deck.

## What the references changed

- **Island position.** The island sits on the starboard edge of the deck, roughly half to two-thirds of the way forward from the stern. Aft to forward the main features are a lattice crane, a white radome, the funnel, a lattice mast with a black upper half and a small dome at the masthead, an air search antenna and the bridge.
- **Ship number.** A white "21" on the island side, and another at the bow reading from astern.
- **Catapult.** One catapult on the port bow, with a bridle catcher horn and a jet blast deflector behind it.
- **Lifts.** Two lifts on the centreline, one forward and one aft.
- **Landing area.** Painted darker than the rest of the deck, with white edge lines, a broad yellow centreline and a dull red foul line. Helicopter spots are numbered 1 to 6.
- **Round-down.** Red and white stripes at the stern.
- **Mirror landing aid.** On a port-side sponson, about amidships.
- **Bow.** Broad and squared off, overhanging a flared hull.

## What references cannot settle

Photographs show how the ship looked on one day. Markings changed, aircraft were repainted, and the angle of a photo hides detail. Where a feature could not be confirmed, the model uses the best-supported version. The post on [what the model still gets wrong](/blog/what-the-model-gets-wrong/) lists those cases.
