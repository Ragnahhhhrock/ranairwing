Mothballed, almost a casino, and the Skyhawks sold to New Zealand.

- 1982-85: moored near Bradley's Head and could have been reactivated in about 26 weeks.
- 30 June 1984: Skyhawks retired; the ten remaining aircraft were sold to the RNZAF in 1984. 31 August 1984: Trackers retired, and the last Tracker flight saluted the carrier.
- 1984: a floating-casino proposal off Eden did not proceed; a A$1.7 million scrap sale fell through in June.

Parts of this account rest on a single source, Wikipedia, listed below. I will update the post if a second source turns up or one of the details is wrong.
