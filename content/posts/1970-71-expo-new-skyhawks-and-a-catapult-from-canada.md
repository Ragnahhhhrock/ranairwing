A busy stretch that doubled the Skyhawk fleet.

- 8 May 1970: Australian National Day at Expo '70, Osaka.
- The second batch of ten A-4Gs was delivered up to July 1971.
- Wikipedia says the catapult was rebuilt in 1971 using parts from HMCS Bonaventure.
- 25 October to 18 November 1971: RIMPAC at Pearl Harbor.

Parts of this account rest on a single source, Wikipedia, listed below. I will update the post if a second source turns up or one of the details is wrong.
