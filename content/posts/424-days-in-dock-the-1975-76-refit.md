Industrial disputes stretched a refit meant to extend her life to at least 1985.

Refit from April 1975; she returned to sea on 18 June 1976 after 424 days.

A Westland Sea King landed on board for the first time on 27 April 1976; the Sea King Mk50 entered service with 817 Squadron in 1976.

**In the sim.** Pedro, the rescue Sea King, now hovers off the port quarter of the carrier in the sim.
