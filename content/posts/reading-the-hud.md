## In the HUD

| Item | Where | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| KNOTS | Left | Indicated airspeed |
| g | Under KNOTS | Load factor |
| AoA | Left, lower | Angle of attack in degrees, with the indexer: amber doughnut on speed, red chevron fast, green chevron slow |
| FEET | Right | Altitude |
| fpm | Under FEET | Climb or descent rate, in feet per minute |
| BALL | Right, lower | Glidepath, only near the ship. See [the ball](/blog/the-ball/) |
| LINED UP | Under the ball | Line-up with the angled deck, with COME LEFT or COME RIGHT |
| nm | Under that | Range to the deck |
| THR and RPM | Bottom centre | Throttle setting, and the engine's actual RPM, which follows the throttle with a lag |
| GEAR, FLAP, HOOK, BRK | Bottom | Lit when gear is down, flaps are out, the hook is down and speedbrakes are out |
| STALL | Centre | Shown above 16.5 degrees of angle of attack |

Messages such as "Launch!", "Trapped: 3 wire" and "Bolter!" appear in the upper centre.

## In the side panels

The panel at bottom left shows the ship:

- **HDG.** The ship's heading, fixed at 250 degrees.
- **SPEED.** Ship speed.
- **WIND.** True wind direction and speed.
- **WIND OVER DECK.** Wind plus ship speed.
- **PITCH, ROLL, HEAVE.** The ship's motion.
- **TRAPS, AIRCRAFT LOST, PILOTS LOST.** Your tally. See [traps, losses and the stats panel](/blog/the-stats-panel/).

## Tips

- On approach, the numbers to watch are KNOTS (or the indexer), fpm and the ball.
- The HUD is drawn in green and amber on purpose, so it stays readable against both sea and sky.
