Remembering the 74 sailors lost from USS Frank E. Evans.

- About 3.15 am in the South China Sea during SEATO Exercise Sea Spirit. Melbourne struck Evans amidships and cut her in two.
- 74 of Evans' 273 crew died; 199 were rescued.
- No. 817 Squadron helicopters rescued 38 American sailors and later received a US Navy Meritorious Unit Commendation.
- Temporary repairs at Singapore from 6 June; back in Sydney 9 July.
