The last full year of service.

- 21 June 1981: rescued 99 Vietnamese refugees in the South China Sea.
- September 1981: the major refit was postponed pending a replacement decision.
- December 1981: more than 180 tons of fresh water accidentally flooded the ship at Garden Island.
