Fifty years ago today, a fire at HMAS Albatross destroyed or damaged most of the RAN's Trackers.

Wikipedia records a deliberately lit fire at HMAS Albatross, Nowra, on 5 December 1976 that destroyed or damaged 12 of the 13 S-2E Trackers.

Fifty years on, this post marks the day and leaves it at that.

Parts of this account rest on a single source, Wikipedia, listed below. I will update the post if a second source turns up or one of the details is wrong.
