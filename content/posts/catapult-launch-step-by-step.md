Press <kbd>R</kbd> to start on the catapult, or <kbd>U</kbd> for the guided version. The aircraft begins in the launch configuration: gear down, flaps half, hook up.

## 1. Run the engine to 100%

Hold <kbd>=</kbd> until RPM reads 100%. If you press <kbd>Space</kbd> early, the sim tells you to run up to full power first. The launch is refused until the engine is above about 85%.

## 2. Launch

Press <kbd>Space</kbd>. The catapult stroke is over in under two seconds. Leave the stick alone while it runs.

## 3. Climb away

Hold <kbd>↓</kbd> for a second or two. The aircraft holds that climb when you let go.

## 4. Raise the gear

Press <kbd>G</kbd> once you are climbing and clear of the ship.

## 5. Retract the flaps

Press <kbd>F</kbd> until the HUD shows FLAP UP. From half, that is two presses: half, full, up.

## 6. Level off

Climb to 1,000 ft, tap <kbd>↑</kbd> to level, and hold <kbd>-</kbd> to bring power back to about 40% so you stop accelerating. Level flight means the climb figure under FEET reads near zero.

## Why a catapult

A carrier's deck is too short for a jet to take off on its own power. The catapult gives it flying speed in a short run. Wind over deck adds to that speed, which is why the ship turns into the wind: see [wind over deck](/blog/wind-over-deck/).

## If nothing happens

Check that RPM is above 85%. Below that the launch is refused.
