The S-2 Tracker is now the second aircraft you can fly. It launches from the same catapult and lands on the same angled deck and wires as the A-4G, but it feels like a different aeroplane.

## Try it

Press <kbd>N</kbd>, or use the Aircraft button in the side panel, to switch between the Skyhawk and the Tracker. The sim restarts you on the catapult in the new aircraft. <kbd>R</kbd> and <kbd>T</kbd> restart on the catapult or on approach in whichever aircraft is selected. Controls are the same as the Skyhawk: <kbd>G</kbd> gear, <kbd>F</kbd> flaps, <kbd>H</kbd> hook.

The guided tutorial is written for the Skyhawk, so starting it switches you back to the A-4G.

## What is different

- **Propellers.** Two propellers that blur into a disc as you add power. Thrust falls away as airspeed rises, so the Tracker accelerates hard at low speed and more slowly at high speed.
- **Slower.** The approach starts at about 101 knots with gear, full flaps and hook down. The Skyhawk's starts at about 123.
- **Gentler catapult.** The shot is a little weaker than the Skyhawk's. With the default ship speed and wind it is enough to fly away; with the ship stopped and no wind it is not.
- **Bigger wing.** Roughly twice the wing area at a heavier weight, so it floats more and bleeds speed differently when you pull power.
- **No speedbrakes.** <kbd>B</kbd> does nothing in the Tracker.
- **Angle of attack.** The indexer uses lower angles than the Skyhawk's. The on-speed circle is about 2.6 to 5.2 degrees.
- **Gear, flaps and hook.** The gear folds back into the engine nacelles, the flaps drop below the wing, and the hook is under the tail.

## Hold on: what is approximate

- The mass, wing area, thrust, drag and stall values are chosen to give plausible handling and a workable catapult shot. They are not taken from a flight manual or a performance chart.
- The side number on the fuselage is a placeholder.
- The model has been checked against the reference photographs only by eye.
- The sound is the same engine sound as the Skyhawk's. It does not yet sound like a piston engine.
- There is no radome, MAD boom animation or weapons bay.

The Tracker that is towed around the deck and taken down the forward lift is a separate, static model with its wings folded.
