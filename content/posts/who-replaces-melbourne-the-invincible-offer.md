Australia agreed to buy HMS Invincible, then the Falklands War changed the maths.

- July 1981: Britain offered Invincible for GBP175 million (A$285 million).
- 25 February 1982: government announced the purchase; she would be renamed HMAS Australia.
- April 1982: the deal was put on hold during the Falklands War; both sides withdrew in July.
- 14 March 1983: Melbourne would not be replaced.
