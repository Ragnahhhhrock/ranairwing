Keys <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>7</kbd> switch cameras. Each one is useful for something different.

| Key | Camera | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>1</kbd> | Orbit | Looking at the whole ship. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | Deck | From the stern end of the flight deck, looking forward along it. |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | Island | From the island, looking aft over the deck toward the wires. |
| <kbd>4</kbd> | Approach | A camera that glides in along the glidepath to the angled deck, then starts over. |
| <kbd>5</kbd> | Flyby | A fixed point out at sea that the ship steams past, zoomed in. |
| <kbd>6</kbd> | Cockpit | The pilot's view with the HUD. Hardest, most rewarding. |
| <kbd>7</kbd> | Chase | Behind the aircraft. Best for learning. |

<kbd>C</kbd> swaps between cockpit and chase.

## Which to fly in

Start in chase. You can see the aircraft's attitude and bank from behind, and the HUD stays on screen. Once landings are steady, move to the cockpit for the pilot's view.

When you restart with <kbd>R</kbd> or <kbd>T</kbd>, the sim moves you to chase unless you are already in a flying camera.

## Camera hints

- The orbit camera stays centred on the ship, not your aircraft. Switch to chase or cockpit when you launch.
- The Approach camera is a good way to see what a correct glidepath looks like before you fly one.
- In the Deck and Island views you can drag to look around.
