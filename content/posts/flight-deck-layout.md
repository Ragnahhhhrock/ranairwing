Use the Island camera (<kbd>3</kbd>) and the Orbit camera (<kbd>1</kbd>) to see the layout.

## What is where

- **Bow.** Broad and squared off, overhanging the hull. "21" is painted here, reading from astern.
- **Catapult.** On the port bow, with a bridle catcher horn and a jet blast deflector behind it. See [the catapult on the port bow](/blog/the-catapult-on-the-port-bow/).
- **Island.** On the starboard edge, roughly half to two-thirds of the way forward from the stern.
- **Parked aircraft.** Three Skyhawks forward of the island, and a Sea King on helicopter spot 1 with its blades folded.
- **Landing area.** The angled deck, painted darker, with white edge lines, a broad yellow centreline and a dull red foul line, and five wires. See [the angled deck](/blog/the-angled-deck/).
- **Round-down.** Red and white stripes at the stern.
- **Mirror landing aid.** On a port-side sponson about amidships.
- **Helicopter spots.** Numbered 1 to 6.
- **Tractors.** Two on the deck.

## Lifts

Two lifts sit on the centreline, one forward and one aft. Their wells are cut through the deck, and the platforms ride down to a hangar deck 7 m below. The forward lift is used by an S-2 Tracker that is towed from its parking spot to the lift, lowered, raised again and towed back, in a loop that takes about 68 seconds.

Press <kbd>L</kbd> to raise or lower the aft lift. It is locked out while aircraft are airborne, because it sits in the landing area. Rolling into an open well is a crash.

## Crew

Figures in coloured jerseys stand at their stations: catapult crew beside the port wingtip, crash crew by the island, tractor drivers, handlers at helicopter spot 1, a lift operator at the well edge, and two figures on the landing signal platform at the stern, facing aft. The colours follow an approximate code that has not been checked against RAN practice.

A second Sea King hovers off the port quarter as plane guard.

## The island, aft to forward

A lattice crane, a white radome, the funnel, a lattice mast with a black upper half and a small dome at the masthead, an air search antenna and the bridge. The island carries a white "21".

## What it leaves out

Markings for individual spots and a simplified hull. See [what the model still gets wrong](/blog/what-the-model-gets-wrong/).
