Melbourne was not scrapped straight away, and her equipment reportedly interested the Chinese navy.

- Chinese naval engineers reportedly studied her under a secret carrier programme. The flight deck and flying equipment were reportedly removed for study, and reports suggest aviators trained on a replica or the deck.
- A 2012 Jane's Navy International article said the equipment likely helped win support for the carrier programme.
- She was rumoured to have been fully broken up as late as 2002. In 2010 she was awarded a Malaysia battle honour.
