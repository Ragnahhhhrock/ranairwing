The biggest and longest RAN deployment since the Second World War.

- Australian Squadron sailed 8 September 1980: Melbourne, Perth, Derwent, Stalwart, Supply and the submarine Otama.
- Response to the Soviet occupation of Afghanistan; three months with visits across the Indian Ocean region.
- Skyhawks were lost on 2 October (Andaman Sea) and 21 October.
- Late October: a Tracker watched Soviet warships shadowing the group.
