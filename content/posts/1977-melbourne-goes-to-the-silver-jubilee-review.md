A long voyage to Britain, a lost helicopter and a Harrier on deck.

- Left 28 April 1977 with HMAS Brisbane and HMNZS Canterbury.
- 9 May: a Sea King was lost in the Indian Ocean; the crew was recovered.
- 25 May: a Tracker located the disabled Dutch vessel Impala Princess in the Gulf of Aden.
- 28 June: represented Australia and New Zealand at the Silver Jubilee Naval Review.
- 30 June: a Harrier demonstrator made trial take-offs and landings aboard.
- Back in Fremantle 19 September and Sydney 4 October 1977.
