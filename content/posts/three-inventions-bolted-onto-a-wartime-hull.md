The angled deck, steam catapult and mirror landing sight changed carrier flying, and Melbourne got all three before she was finished.

Modifications during completion in the early 1950s: an angled flight deck (5.5 degrees), a steam catapult and a mirror landing aid; larger lifts were fitted when work resumed.

Delays and the added systems pushed the cost to AU£8.3 million.

**In the sim.** The sim models a 5.5 degree angled deck, a catapult on the port bow and a mirror landing aid on a port-side sponson. The deck geometry is still approximate.
